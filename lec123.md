Purpose and transcription conventions
- Slide text is transcribed in slide order, with line breaks normalized where helpful.
- Original terminology, names, spelling, capitalization, and apparent typographical errors are retained when readable. Mathematical notation and GAMS code are represented in plain text.
- “Visual description” records the visible layout, diagram structure, screenshot, chart, or illustration so that a reader who does not have the PDF can understand what appeared on the slide.
- Some diagrams contain bilingual Arabic/English labels. English labels are transcribed where legible; Arabic labels are described or translated only where their meaning is clear from the adjacent English text.
- The files are lecture slides, not prose documents. A slide with little or no extracted body text may still contain a title, diagram, or visual-only content; those are described below.
