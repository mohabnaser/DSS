Decision Support Systems (DSS)
• Model-oriented tools.
• Strong analytical features.
• Context-specific with respect to decision problem.
• Used by middle/ Senior management for semi-structured decisions.
• Internal and external Information.

--- Slide 8 ---
Executive Information Systems (EIS)
• Support decision-making activities of top/ Senior management level (executives).
• Fast response time.
• External and internal information.
• Used for strategic planning and long term decisions.
• Can represent Front-end for DSS.
• Simple but powerful analytical features, it is based mainly on Expert Opinion and Knowledge .

--- Slide 11 ---
Computer Aided Expert Systems (ES)
• Add knowledge and knowledge management to support the decision making process (DSS).
• problem-solving analytical software system that Attempt to mimic (or simulate) human expertise.
• Needs a knowledge base to store decision rules.
• Can reach a level of performance comparable to or even exceeding human expertise in some specialized problem areas (as they store collective expertise).

--- Slide 12 ---
Title: Expert System (Classical AI, Rule-based AI, Good-Old-Fashioned AI (GOFAI))
[Image Description: An architecture diagram of an Expert System. Text Bullet Points:
• It is part of AI family
• It uses expert knowledge to support decision making process.
• It deals with both quantitative and qualitative data.
• It uses facts and rules to explain human reasoning and expertise.
• It relies on acquisition, representation of knowledge (to construct a Rule-based knowledge Structure)
• Execution is done using heuristics / logic. Knowledge retrieval and draws of conclusions are done using an: "inference engine sub-system"
• Include explanatory & knowledge refinement facilities
